WHO WE ARE

Patient capital.
Purposeful ownership.

Karana Investment Group is a privately held investment organization focused on acquiring and supporting established businesses and real assets throughout the United States.

We work directly with business owners, intermediaries, lenders, operators, and aligned capital partners. Our approach emphasizes thoughtful diligence, flexible transaction structures, operational continuity, and long-term value creation.
